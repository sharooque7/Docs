### Feature Scaling
* Applies to column only

#### Normalization

X' = X - Xmin / Xmax - Xmin
[0,1]

#### Standardization

X' = X - u / p

u - averga
p - standard deviation

[-3, 3]

### Example



| User | Salary | Normalization | Age | Normalization |
| ---- | ------ | ------------- | --- | ------------- |
| U1   | 70000  | 1             | 45  |  1            |
| U2   | 60000  | 0.444         | 44  | 0.8           |
| U3   | 50000  | 0             | 40  | 0             |
